# 📊 PR Supermarket – Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed to analyze and visualize sales and profitability performance for **PR Supermarket**.

The project transforms retail sales data into meaningful business insights using interactive KPIs, charts, filters, and geographical analysis.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to analyze retail sales performance across different **categories, sub-categories, regions, states, and months**.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key business metrics and allows users to interactively explore performance using **Year, Category, and Region filters**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall sales performance
* Track maximum yearly profit
* Analyze sales by sub-category
* Compare profit across product categories
* Analyze quantity sold by category
* Identify regional sales distribution
* Track monthly sales trends
* Analyze state-wise sales and profit
* Build an interactive business intelligence dashboard

## 🔍 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Sales by Sub-Category

The dashboard compares sales across major sub-categories such as:

* Phones
* Storage
* Tables
* Paper
* Supplies

The visualization helps identify which sub-categories contribute more to overall sales.

### 2. Profit by Category

Profitability is analyzed across:

* Technology
* Office Supplies
* Furniture

This provides a clear comparison of category-level contribution to overall profit.

### 3. Quantity by Category

The dashboard analyzes the quantity sold across different product categories and sub-categories to understand product demand.

### 4. Sales by Region

Regional sales performance is visualized using an interactive donut chart, allowing comparison between:

* East
* Central
* South
* West

### 5. Monthly Sales Trend

A monthly line chart is used to analyze sales performance throughout the year and identify changes in sales over time.

### 6. State-wise Sales & Profit

The geographical map visualizes sales and profit distribution across different states, providing a regional perspective of business performance.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive filters for:

* **Year:** 2019, 2020
* **Category:** Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology
* **Region:** Central, East, South, West

These filters allow users to dynamically explore different segments of the dataset.
